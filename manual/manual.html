01Welcome

Most groove generators randomise which notes play. GLAPP decides when they land.

The feel of Romanian minimal and the Berlin minimal school lives in milliseconds: a rim that hangs 20 ms behind the beat, a percussion loop of 13 steps that never lines up with the bar, a ghost note that only exists because two sounds wanted the same spot. GLAPP is built for exactly that. It sends MIDI to BETONG, a drum machine, a sampler or a synth, and every note is placed with sample accuracy.

  • Eight polyrhythmic lanes, each with its own length (1 to 32 steps) and clock (1/4 to 1/32, including triplets).
  • DRAG, INERTIA and JITTER per lane: late, sticky and human.
  • ORBIT makes lanes wander over time, so nothing loops exactly the same.
  • EVENTS on the last beat of each phrase: silence, a hiccup, a ratchet or a skipped beat.
  • INTERLOCK stops two hits fighting for the same spot.
  • The dice follows the rules of a style. It never spits out chaos.
  • 200 presets in four styles.

02Install and connect

macOS: unzip GLAPP-macOS.zip from your Downloads folder, open Terminal and run:

bash ~/Downloads/GLAPP/installera.command

Quit your DAW completely and start it again. GLAPP shows up among the instruments. Requires macOS 11 or newer, Intel or Apple Silicon.

Windows: unzip GLAPP-Windows.zip and copy GLAPP.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

Connecting GLAPP to a sound

GLAPP is an instrument that outputs MIDI. By default lane 1 to 8 play C1 to G1 (notes 36 to 43) on channel 1, which are pads 1 to 8 in BETONG.

  1. Bitwig: put GLAPP on an instrument track and BETONG (or any drum machine) right after it in the same device chain. Bitwig passes GLAPP's notes on to the next device.
  2. If your Bitwig version does not pass the notes on: put BETONG on its own track and choose GLAPP's track as its note input (or use a Note Receiver device).
  3. Ableton Live: put GLAPP on a MIDI track. On a second MIDI track with your drums, set "MIDI From" to the GLAPP track and choose GLAPP in the lower box. Arm the second track or set monitoring to In.
  4. Logic: use the AU version and route its MIDI output with the environment, or use the VST3 in another host.

Want to hear GLAPP on its own? Press PROVLJUD. A tiny built-in drum kit plays along. Turn it off when the real sounds take over.

03Your first minute

  1. Press play in your DAW. GLAPP follows the DAW's tempo and play/stop. No DAW running? Press SPELA to use GLAPP's own clock and TEMPO knob.
  2. Step through presets with the arrows, or click the name to pick from all 200.
  3. Pick a STIL and hit DICE · MUTERA until something grabs you.
  4. FRYS the lanes you love. Roll again: only the others change.
  5. Press ORBIT EVOLVE to keep the pattern but change how it moves over time.
  6. Went too far? ÅNGRA goes back, up to 40 steps.

04The eight lanes

Each lane has a role. The role decides how the dice treats it, and the order decides who wins in INTERLOCK (higher up wins).

The step grid

Click a step to turn it on or off. Drag up or down for velocity. Scroll over a step to change its pitch in semitones (shown on the bar). Shift-click makes a ghost note. Right-click clears the step. The thin white tick on each hit shows where it really lands after drag and inertia.

On each lane: LÄNGD, DRAG, TRÖGHET, JITTER, FRYS, TYST and a dice for that lane only. Click a lane's name to open it in the VERKSTAD below, which holds clock, note, channel, level, chance, note length, orbit, events and chord.

URVERKET

The clockwork on the right shows every lane as a ring, the kick innermost. Hits are dots, nudged by their drag. Each lane has its own play head going round at its own speed, so you can literally watch the lanes slide apart and meet again.

05Time: DRAG, INERTIA, JITTER

DRAG (0 to 60 ms) makes a lane hit late. Late, never early: a note cannot be sent before it happens, and the pocket is behind the beat anyway.

TRÖGHET (inertia) adds delay when hits come in a row: the second hit gets +12 ms, the third +24, the fourth +38. Fast runs sound like they slip on an oil stain.

JITTER (0 to 5 ms) adds a little human randomness. It is the same every time the song passes that spot, so exports and loops stay stable.

In GROOVE: SLÄP scales all drag on all lanes (0 % is dead straight, 200 % is very loose). HUMAN scales all jitter. FÖLJ TEMPO: drag is set for 122 BPM and follows the tempo, so the feel stays the same at 118 or 128. Turn it off for fixed milliseconds.

06Movement: ORBIT and EVENTS

ORBIT makes a lane move over time:

  • STEG + turns the pattern one step later every N bars. STEG − one step earlier.
  • GLID lets the drag breathe: 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 ms and back again, one notch every N bars.

HÄNDELSE (event) happens on the last beat of each phrase (FRAS = 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars):

  • TYST silences the lane for the whole last bar.
  • HICKA throws the last beat's hits 45 ms late: the hiccup that makes the floor lose its jaw.
  • RATCHET turns those hits into fast triple strikes.
  • HOPP skips the last beat, a small hole before the one.

07INTERLOCK

With INTERLOCK on, two lanes never hit at exactly the same moment. The lane higher up wins and the kick always wins. What happens to the loser is set in INTERLOCK LÄGE:

  • SPÖKE: it plays as a ghost note, velocity 18 to 35.
  • FLYTTA: it is pushed to a late 32nd after the beat.
  • TYST: it is dropped.

08The dice, styles and presets

Every dice in GLAPP works within the rules of the chosen STIL. It never touches NOT or KANAL, because those are your connection to the drums.

StyleCharacter
BUKARESTRolling, woody, very late ghost notes, 15- and 23-step percussion cycles. Sometimes the chord lane stays silent on purpose.
BERLINCrooked clicks, endless phase drift, odd hat cycles, triplet percussion, tighter drag.
MIKRODUBSloppy backbeats, sparse 13-step accents, dub chords, extra drag.
ORGANISKWooden clappers, euclidean kicks now and then, stumbling micro-grooves.

Density is always low: percussion lanes get 2 to 5 hits per 16 steps. Minimal is built on silence.

Presets: 200, fifty per style. Each preset is a fixed seed for the dice, so it always comes back the same. Frozen lanes are left alone when you change preset, so you can keep your kick and rim and browse the rest.

09Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens: is the DAW playing? Without a DAW, press SPELA. Turn on PROVLJUD to check that GLAPP itself is running.
  • The drums get no notes: see "Connecting GLAPP to a sound" in chapter 02. Check that NOT and KANAL match your drum machine.
  • macOS says the plugin is damaged: run installera.command again. It removes the quarantine flag.
  • It sounds too loose: turn SLÄP down, or freeze what works and roll the rest.
